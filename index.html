Questions people ask

Are these apps free?

The ones listed are free to use. No need to pay any money to any app

Why no modded or “premium unlocked” apps?

Modified copies of paid apps are usually pirated, and they’re a common way malware spreads. This site only links to official and open-source releases.

How do I know a download is genuine?

Download from the project’s own site or repository, and compare the checksum if one is published. If a site is copying the app’s name but not its address, leave.
